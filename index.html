Calculation:

Total Sarsai = (Kanal × 20 × 9) + (Marla × 9) + Sarsai.

Individual ownership = Total Sarsai × (Hissa numerator ÷ Hissa denominator).

Ownership % = Hissa numerator ÷ Hissa denominator × 100.

Unutilised % = Unallocated Sarsai ÷ Total Sarsai × 100.
